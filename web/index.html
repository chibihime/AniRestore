About this demo
  • Runs the exported ONNX model with ONNX Runtime Web, on WebGPU when your browser supports it, otherwise on CPU via WebAssembly. Large inputs are downscaled first (longest side 512px on WebGPU, 256px on CPU, where a restore takes around 30 seconds).
  • The project's custom fused Triton kernel is a CUDA kernel and can't run in a browser; its benchmark results (Tesla T4) are in the README.
  • Model: Real-ESRGAN by Xintao Wang et al., BSD-3-Clause. Sample images are copyrighted cover art, shown for non-commercial demonstration only.
